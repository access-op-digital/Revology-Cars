Tom Scarpello

Founder & CEO, Revology Cars

Tom is the founder and CEO of Revology Cars, and the person whose standards the Orlando factory is set up to hold. He has spent his entire career in the automotive industry, with a résumé covering manufacturing, marketing, sales and product planning for Ford and Nissan, in the United States and overseas.

Seventeen of those years were at Ford, starting on the plant floor as a production supervisor in 1988 and moving through research, media and strategic planning roles before leading marketing and sales for the Special Vehicle Team from 1998 to 2004, where he was product manager for the first Ford GT supercar. He went on to serve as vice president of marketing at Jaguar, then spent seven years with Nissan and Infiniti in roles spanning project leader, general manager, regional vice president and director, work that took him through California, Mexico, Hong Kong and Japan. He holds an MBA from Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management and a business degree from the University of Georgia.

Scarpello had spent over three decades inside the process an automaker uses to develop a car: platform planning, system integration, validation, serviceability, continuous improvement. When he founded Revology in 2014, he applied that process to a 1960s Mustang instead of buying one and modifying it. Today, Tom Scarpello continues to guide Revology Cars’ engineering philosophy, vehicle specifications, and manufacturing standards from the company’s Orlando, Florida facility, where the team builds modern interpretations of iconic 1967–1970 Mustang models.

From Ford Executive to Revology Founder

Tom Scarpello’s automotive career spans decades of leadership across vehicle development, performance programs, and global automotive operations. The experience gained at Ford, Jaguar, Nissan, and Infiniti shaped the engineering philosophy behind Revology Cars.

Ford Motor Company

Tom began his automotive career at Ford Motor Company on the plant floor as a production supervisor in 1988. After earning his MBA, he returned to Ford and held roles across research, strategic planning, marketing, and product development, including SVT Marketing and Sales Manager. Two of those years were spent at Ford México as marketing manager.

Ford Special Vehicle Team

For six years he led marketing and sales for SVT, the group responsible for Ford’s factory performance cars, and served as product manager for the first Ford GT supercar. It is the part of the résumé that matters most to a Revology: SVT existed to take a production platform and re-engineer it as a complete, integrated performance vehicle.

Jaguar, Nissan and Infiniti

Tom continued his automotive career as vice president of marketing at Jaguar, then general manager of commercial trucks at Tuttle-Click, founded his own venture in Mexico, then spent seven years with Nissan and Infiniti as project leader, general manager, regional vice president and director. The work took him through California, Mexico, Hong Kong and Japan.

Revology Cars founded

He founded Revology in Orlando, Florida, applying the platform-based development process he had spent decades inside to a 1960s Mustang instead of buying one and modifying it. The company has designed and built reproduction 1967–1970 Mustangs and Shelby GTs there ever since, for owners in twenty countries across four continents.

Skills and Expertise Behind Tom’s Vision

Tom Scarpello is the architect of Revology’s OEM-style approach to building reproduction classic cars: developing a 1960s Mustang as a complete modern vehicle through platform development, system integration, and manufacturing at specialty volumes, applying the automaker process he worked inside for seventeen years.

Reproduction Vehicle Engineering

Tom applies a platform-based approach to developing 1967–1970 Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles. Instead of creating one-off modifications, he focuses on integrating modern systems and components into a complete, engineered automobile.

OEM Product Development

Tom brings experience from inside major automotive organizations, applying principles such as platform planning, system integration, validation, serviceability, and continuous improvement to the development of specialty vehicles.

Performance Vehicle Programs

Tom’s experience with Ford’s Special Vehicle Team included leading marketing and sales for factory performance vehicles and serving as product manager for the first Ford GT, connecting engineering, performance, and product strategy.

Low-Volume Manufacturing

Tom helped establish Revology’s Orlando manufacturing facility, where engineering, body fabrication, paint, trim, final assembly, and quality validation are integrated into a complete vehicle production process.

Product Planning, Marketing & Sales

Tom’s career includes leadership roles across product planning, marketing, and sales at Ford and Nissan. His experience spans U.S. and international automotive markets, combining business strategy with vehicle development.

Classic Mustang & Shelby GT Heritage

Tom understands the design details that define classic Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles. His approach preserves the styling, character, and heritage of these iconic models while adapting them with modern engineering and technology.
