About Revology: Redefining the Classic Mustang

Revology Cars builds reproduction Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles that combine the timeless design of classic Ford models with modern engineering, technology, and craftsmanship. Founded by Tom Scarpello and based in Orlando, Florida, Revology creates modern interpretations of iconic Mustang platforms while preserving the character of the originals.

The Origin Story of Revology Cars

“This is my life’s work. Everything I have learned in almost 35 years in the automotive industry, rolled up into one very special line of products.”

The story of Revology Cars began with Tom Scarpello’s passion for the original Mustang and his extensive experience in the automotive industry. Tom Scarpello spent 17 years working at Ford, running the Special Vehicle Team from 1998 to 2004, and kept hearing the same question at auto shows: “Why doesn’t Ford bring back the original Mustang?”

While later Mustang generations incorporated styling influences from the original 1960s models, Scarpello recognized that many enthusiasts continued to value the authenticity and character of the original Mustang design. This inspired the idea behind Revology Cars: creating a reproduction Mustang that preserved classic styling while incorporating modern engineering, technology, and vehicle systems.

Founded in 2014, Revology Cars was built around the belief that a modern classic vehicle requires more than updated components. Changes to one vehicle system can affect others, making proper engineering and system integration essential for performance, reliability, comfort, and safety.

The result was the Revology Mustang, a modern interpretation of the classic Mustang developed with contemporary systems and components while maintaining the design heritage and personality of the original. Based in Orlando, Florida, Revology Cars continues to design and manufacture reproduction Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles for enthusiasts who appreciate classic automotive design combined with modern functionality.

Re-Engineering Classic Mustangs With Modern Technology

Every Revology vehicle is developed from a dedicated vehicle platform rather than created as a one-off modification. Revology Cars engineers each platform to support the integration of modern automotive systems and components, creating reproduction Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles that are designed as complete automobiles. This platform-based approach enables consistency, continuous refinement, and the integration of classic Mustang design with modern engineering.

Engineered Like an Automaker, Not a Shop

We apply an OEM-style approach to developing classic Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles. Instead of building each vehicle independently, we develop a platform designed to integrate modern automotive systems while preserving the styling, character, and heritage of the original Mustang.

This engineering process focuses on system integration, reliability, serviceability, and continuous improvement. The result is a modern classic Mustang designed to deliver the craftsmanship of a specialty automobile with the functionality expected from a contemporary vehicle.

Designed for the Driving Experience

A great driving experience depends on more than horsepower alone. We engineer the relationship between the vehicle’s systems, including suspension, steering, braking, chassis, tires, and powertrain, so they work together as a complete package.

By combining classic Mustang design with modern engineering principles, Revology creates vehicles that deliver the character of an iconic automobile with the confidence, comfort, and drivability of a modern performance vehicle.

Automotive Engineering and Manufacturing at Revology

Revology Cars applies automotive engineering principles to the development and manufacturing of modern classic Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles. Our process goes beyond assembling individual components by using structured development methods, engineering validation, and manufacturing controls to create complete, repeatable automobiles.

Our engineering team uses processes such as Production Part Approval Process (PPAP), Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA), tooling and fixture development, and Statistical Process Control (SPC) to support quality, consistency, and manufacturing precision. These automotive quality methods help identify potential issues, validate processes, and maintain control throughout vehicle development and production.

Since beginning production in 2014, we have continuously refined the Revology vehicle platform through ongoing engineering improvements. Over time, hundreds of functional improvements have been incorporated into the platform, enhancing areas such as reliability, serviceability, performance, and overall vehicle refinement.

From our Orlando, Florida manufacturing facility, we design and build reproduction Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles using a platform-based approach that allows each automobile to benefit from continuous development while preserving the classic Mustang character.

Our Commitment to Quality, Performance & Authenticity

Revology Cars focuses on creating modern interpretations of classic Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles through careful engineering, craftsmanship, and attention to detail. Our approach combines the heritage and styling of iconic Mustang models with modern systems, components, and manufacturing practices.

Powertrain

The Ford 5.0L Ti-VCT DOHC “Coyote” V8 at 460 hp, or the Ford/ROUSH Performance supercharged and intercooled version at 710 hp. Engines are sourced directly from Ford Motor Company, delivered in the same reusable dunnage Ford uses to ship engines to its own Flat Rock Assembly Plant.

Authenticity

A Revology is restored from an original donor Mustang to Revology’s specifications, then sold and titled as a used vehicle, the same way as any other used vehicle. It may be registered in California on that same basis.

Licensing

Revology was the first company ever authorized by Ford to build new reproduction 1965–1970 Mustang rolling chassis, under license from 2015 to 2024. When Ford changed its licensing policy for road-going vehicles, Revology shifted to restoring donor cars to the same specification, and continues to work closely with Ford on other projects. Revology remains in good standing with Shelby American and is one of only four companies worldwide authorized to build licensed reproduction Shelby GTs.

Safety engineering

The platform carries passive safety improvements the original cars never had, including three-point driver and passenger seat belts, a dual-circuit braking system, a collapsible steering shaft, and a fuel-pump inertia switch.

Warranty

The “1-2-5” New Vehicle Limited Warranty: 1 year bumper-to-bumper, 2 years powertrain, 5 years body corrosion coverage, with no mileage limit on any of it. Warranty work can be completed at any participating Ford dealership in the U.S.

Serviceability

A Revology Mustang can be serviced at any Ford dealership, and at virtually any independent shop capable of servicing both early and late-model Mustangs. That is a deliberate outcome of the OEM-component strategy, not a coincidence.

Value retention

Most Revology Mustangs and Shelby GTs sell through the Certified Pre-Owned program and typically retain 90–110% of their original price after two years and 2,500 miles. Resale value is a function of supply and demand, and Revology will always be demand-driven, not supply-driven.
