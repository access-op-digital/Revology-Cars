Revology Cars In Brief

Revology Cars builds reproduction Mustang and Shelby GT vehicles inspired by iconic 1967–1970 Mustang designs, combining classic Ford styling with modern engineering and craftsmanship. Founded in 2014 by Tom Scarpello, Revology develops each vehicle from an engineered platform using an OEM-inspired approach, preserving the character of classic Mustangs while integrating modern systems and technology.

Every vehicle is designed and built at the company’s headquarters and manufacturing facility on Commerce Park Drive in Orlando, Florida, where engineering, body fabrication, paint, trim, final assembly and quality validation happen under one roof. Around 170 people work there. Cars have gone to customers in the United States and nineteen other countries.

Ford authorized Revology to build reproduction 1965–1970 Mustang rolling chassis from 2015 to 2024, the first company to hold such a license; Revology now restores donor cars to the same specification instead.

“This is my life’s work. Everything I have learned in almost 35 years in the automotive industry, rolled up into one very special line of products.”
Tom Scarpello Founder & CEO, Revology Cars
