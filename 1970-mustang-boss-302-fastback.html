Ford® introduced the Boss 302 for the 1969 model year to homologate a high-performance engine to remain competitive in the Trans American Sedan Championship, better known as Trans-Am. The Boss 302 engine combined a Windsor small block bottom end with Cleveland-style heads, creating a high-revving engine to compete with Chevrolet’s Z/28. While 1969 was essentially a development year for the new Boss against the proven Z/28, in 1970 the Ford teams secured six wins out of eleven races with Parnelli Jones winning the Trans Am title driving a Bud Moore-prepared Boss 302. The production Boss 302 also continued in 1970 with several refinements and styling updates. Due to its uniqueness and exclusivity, the Boss 302 Mustang model is sought after by collectors and performance enthusiasts. The Revology 1970 Mustang Boss 302 Fastback is a re-engineered restomod of Ford’s iconic Trans-Am racing-inspired Mustang, built new with a 460-horsepower Ford 5.0L V8, a reinforced all-steel unibody, modern suspension and braking systems, and premium interior craftsmanship.

Performance

Engine

The standard engine in the Revology Boss 302 is the Ford 5.0L Ti-VCT DOHC V8, producing 460 horsepower. The engine delivers effortless performance when called upon, yet remains smooth, refined, and easy to drive in everyday traffic.

Transmission

Choose between a six-speed manual and an electronically controlled ten-speed automatic transmission, both with overdrive for effortless highway cruising and excellent fuel mileage.

Performance Brake Package

The Mustang GT is equipped with high performance power brakes featuring 12.88” slotted and ventilated rotors w/ 6 piston front and 4 piston rear calipers.

Carbon Fiber Driveshaft

Carbon fiber is the choice for high performance driveline applications due to its high strength and light weight, but it is also more forgiving than steel or aluminum, which serves to reduce driveline harshness, improving the overall level of refinement of the vehicle and making it more enjoyable to drive.

Exhaust System

The stainless steel dual exhaust system is built by Borla to our specification. Tuned specifically for the Ford Coyote engine, it delivers a soft burble at idle and is quiet with no drone at cruise speed.

Rear Axle Assembly

All Revology Mustangs and Shelby GTs are equipped with a Ford 8.8″ rear end and Traction-Lok limited slip differential for quiet operation with superior durability. Unlike aftermarket gear sets, the 3.31 (A/T) and 3.73 (M/T) gear sets are Ford engineered and built at Ford’s Sterling Axle plant. Heavy duty 31-spline axles reliably transfer the car’s prodigious power to the pavement. ​

Features

Functional

Equipped with an aluminum subframe, precisely tuned double wishbone suspension, power hydraulic rack and pinion steering, and springs and shocks tuned to Revology specifications to deliver the ideal balance of handling and ride comfort.

Interior

The Revology Mustang front seats provide improved comfort and lateral support over the original seats, while maintaining an authentic appearance. Based on a current OEM seat, the front seats feature unique Revology-designed foam and upholstery combined with a robust and lightweight frame and mechanicals for the ultimate in comfort, functionality, and authenticity. Front seats are equipped with manual fore/aft and recline. Seats and interior trim panels are covered in premium OEM automotive grade vinyl or OEM automotive leather which offers superior abrasion resistance and protection from sun loading than aftermarket materials.

Exterior

LED reverse and parking lamps, tail lamps w/sequential turn signals, exterior door handle courtesy lamps, and optional LED headlamps provide improved visibility at night as well as a distinctive appearance, day or night. The Revology paint process consists of an epoxy primer, urethane primer, basecoat, and clearcoat. We use exclusively Glasurit automotive paint finishes for the ultimate in appearance and durability. Contemporary OEM colors from Ferrari, Porsche, Lexus, and others also available.

Structural Adhesive

Revology Cars assembles its Mustang body structures with new parts, using production fixtures designed in CAD for superior dimensional control and modern automated welding equipment for consistent weld integrity. In addition, we apply modern structural adhesives to all structural weld flanges during assembly then bake the body at 350°F to cure the adhesives, stiffening the body from 40-60%. Structural adhesives not only increase stiffness, which eliminates the squeaks and rattles common in a 60-year old automobile body, they also improve corrosion resistance, long-term durability, and contribute to improved crash performance. Until now, this technology was only available on new vehicles from major OEMs, such as BMW, Ford, and Honda. Revology is the first and presently the only company in this segment to adopt this advanced technology.

CAN Bus Electrical

OEM-style approach with centralized control module, enabling integration of lighting, windows, mirrors, sensors, and software updates.

Modern Power Windows

Reliable modern OEM power window mechanism with one-touch-down functionality to enhance usability.

Power Rear View Mirrors

A luxury upgrade that comes standard with the Boss—fully integrated into our electrical system architecture.

Hood Hinges

All Revology Mustangs and Shelby GTs feature aluminum hood hinges with nitrogen filled struts for stable, quiet, and reliable operation—a notable improvement over the wobbly, squeaky 1960s hinges.

Doors

Revology Mustangs and Shelby GTs feature modern door hinges and door latch mechanisms. While it doesn’t sound very sexy, these modern components make the car much more enjoyable to use. No more squeaks and creaks as you open the doors—they open smoothly and quietly, and close with a solid “thunk”. When closed, the precisely latched doors contribute to the overall rigidity of the body.

Polyurethane Bonded Windshield and Backlite Glass

All Revology Mustangs and Shelby GTs come standard with polyurethane bonded windshield and backlite (fastback models) glass. Polyurethane bonding is the way all modern automotive glass is secured not only because it seals better than rubber but also because it makes the glass part of the body structure, increasing body rigidity. Increased rigidity reduces noise, vibration, and harshness, but most importantly improves safety, as the stiffer roof is less likely to crush in a roll over and the glass won’t pop out and allow passengers to be ejected in a collision.

100% Steel Unibody

Modern cars all feature unibody, or monocoque, construction because it is stiffer and lighter than body on frame. For a unibody vehicle, the body provides the structure for the powertrain and chassis, which makes it much more important that it is designed and built properly. To ensure the structural rigidity and tight tolerances that allow the packaging of powerful new engines, we reengineered the Mustang unibody with added structural components and additional welds for increased strength. We build all Revology unibodies in-house, using locating fixtures, jigs, and templates to ensure proper dimensional control and sophisticated automated spot-welding equipment that senses the thickness and composition of metal that is being welded and applies the proper amount of current to ensure good quality welds.

Charging System

A convenient 4.3A on board trickle charger connects via a magnetic contact under the rear bumper to keep your battery charged even while the car is not driven for long periods.

Safety

Three point front seat belts, a dual circuit braking system, fuel shutoff inertia switch, collapsible steering shaft, and optional front seat head restraints help protect occupants in the event of a collision.

Entertainment Systems

Optional Harman-Kardon premium audio system exclusive to and calibrated specifically for the Revology Boss interior features 6.5" glass-fiber woofers and PEI polymer tweeters in the door panels and rear package shelf, controlled by an A2b Digital Sound Processor and powered by an 800w RMS 8 channel amplifier, complemented by a 10" trunk-mounted subwoofer in a sealed enclosure powered by a separate 1000w RMS 1 channel amplifier.

Craftsmanship

Leather and Alcantara™ Trim Packages

Every Revology interior is meticulously cut, sewn, and hand-fitted by skilled craftsmen. From the seats and floor mats to the dashboard, door panels, quarter trim panels, and sun visors, each component is individually crafted to deliver exceptional fit, finish, and long-term durability. This traditional, handcrafted approach also provides the flexibility to personalize materials and colors to each owner's preferences. The interior is trimmed in premium OEM-grade enhanced-grain Nappa leather throughout the front and rear seats, door panels, instrument panel, center console, quarter trim panels, headliner, and kick panels, creating a refined cabin with the look, feel, and durability expected of a modern luxury automobile. Available interior enhancements include contrast piping, genuine Alcantara® seat inserts, Revology Woven Leather seat inserts, and an Alcantara® headliner and sun visors for an even more distinctive and luxurious cabin.

Steering Wheel

A leather-wrapped ode to the original Boss steering wheel, upgraded with contemporary rim thickness and thumb rests for a confident drive.

Instrument Panel Design

Designed and manufactured to Revology specifications, featuring leather-wrapped upper and lower surfaces accented by genuine flat-cut Black American Walnut veneers with integrated controls and a hidden 7-inch touchscreen.

Console

Thoughtful blend of craftsmanship and utility, including hidden storage, removable cup holders, and a USB port.

Pedal Package

Revised floor structure and pedal placement—including an all-new clutch assembly—optimized for control and legroom.

Fit and Finish

The Revology Mustang body is assembled with all-new steel panels. These panels are painstakingly fit by hand to ensure exceptional fit and flushness.
